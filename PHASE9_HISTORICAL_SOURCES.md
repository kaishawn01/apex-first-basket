**No compatible, permitted, zero-new-cost end-to-end replacement was established.** This is a scoped search result, not a claim that no such source can exist. The Phase 8 thirteen-candidate audit is preserved; this phase follows the most promising routes and adds one provenance review. No sports dataset was downloaded, no API plan changed, and no permission request sent.

Ranked findings are in [PHASE9_SOURCE_AUDIT.json](validation/PHASE9_SOURCE_AUDIT.json). Ranking favors demonstrated permission and then actual opening events, compatibility, coverage, reliability, cost and setup; no candidate satisfies every condition.

1. Existing BALLDONTLIE access is the shortest inspection path. Its [terms](https://www.balldontlie.io/terms.html) permit lawful analytical/model use of legitimately obtained data, with restrictions. Those terms do not grant additional endpoint entitlement or authorize a different provider's archive. No separate permission inquiry is needed for uses already covered by those terms. We have not verified this account.
2. [SportsDataIO](https://sportsdata.io/help/data-rights-and-licensing-questions) distinguishes personal free Discovery use from contract-specific public/commercial rights. Free NBA opening-event coverage is unverified. Ask about a scoped research grant before registering or collecting. A documentation-page timeout in this review is a retrieval failure, not proof of denial.
3. Independent observations could support future collection if provenance and permission are documented. No such dataset was supplied; this is not a substitute for the missing 400-day empirical history.
4. The [NBA archive dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cdechoch/nba-data-archive/blob/6bbd59b169c6d5ae9f3e48803145cd6b6ff39fa6/README.md) describes a Parquet mirror of NBA/pbpstats-derived records under an Apache label.
